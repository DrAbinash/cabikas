About

Big-firm rigour.
Personal, hometown service.

Numbers tell the story of every business and every family’s hard work. My practice is built on a simple belief: that people in Deoghar and the wider Santhal Pargana region deserve the same quality of professional advice that large corporations receive in metro cities — delivered with patience, in plain language, and with complete integrity.

After graduating with B.Com (Hons) from Shri Ram College of Commerce and qualifying as a Chartered Accountant, I trained at A. F. Ferguson & Co., where I learned the discipline of thorough audit, careful documentation and uncompromising ethics. I bring that discipline to every engagement — whether it is an individual’s income-tax return, a trader’s GST compliance, or the statutory audit of a growing company.

What you can expect

  • Straightforward advice in plain language — Hindi or English, as you prefer
  • Deadlines tracked and met, so you avoid interest, late fees and notices
  • Careful documentation that stands up to scrutiny
  • Complete confidentiality of your financial information
  • Direct access to the Chartered Accountant — not just to staff

Services

Everything your compliance
needs, under one roof.

Income Tax

Return filing for individuals, firms and companies, tax planning within the law, TDS compliance, advance tax, refunds, and representation in assessments and appeals.

GST

Registration, monthly and annual returns, input-tax-credit reconciliation, e-way bill and e-invoicing guidance, refunds, notices and departmental representation.

Audit & Assurance

Statutory audit, tax audit under Section 44AB, internal audit, stock and bank audits — conducted with the thoroughness of big-firm methodology.

Company & LLP

Incorporation of companies and LLPs, ROC annual filings, board and statutory registers, DIN/DSC, and ongoing MCA compliance so your entity stays in good standing.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Clean, audit-ready books maintained on Tally or cloud platforms, payroll, MIS reports and financial statements that help you actually understand your business.

Registrations & Licences

PAN, TAN, MSME/Udyam, professional tax, trade licences, 12A/80G for charitable trusts, and the registrations a new venture needs to begin on the right footing.

Project Finance

Project reports, CMA data and financial projections for bank loans, working-capital limits and government subsidy schemes — presented the way bankers expect.

Business Advisory

Startup structuring, certification services (net worth, turnover, visa/foreign remittance), succession planning and practical counsel for growing family businesses.
